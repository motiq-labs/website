Motiq selected as an AI-BOOST Spark phase winner

Motiq is among the Spark phase winners of the AI-BOOST AI Challenge Competition. We now move into the Advance phase to develop and validate our approach to language-based mission planning for autonomous agricultural robots.

Natural language, practical robot tasks

Our work addresses Challenge 1: using generative AI to turn natural-language instructions into structured missions for autonomous agricultural robots. The aim is to let people describe a task in text or speech, then translate that request into a plan the robot can carry out, with checks before execution.

What comes next

During Advance, we will develop and test a proof of concept in collaboration with Consorzio Intellimech and JoiiNT LAB, focusing on agricultural use cases and validation in a vineyard environment.

Prototype development and validation are ongoing. Testing on the target robot is still ahead.
